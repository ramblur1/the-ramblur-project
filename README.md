# the-ramblur-project

README - billion dollar platform project.

<h3>Title:</h3>

The Ramblur Project

<h3>About the project:</h3>

In 2015 a web project was started to build a forum based social media platform aimed to achieve a fresh approach to traditional forum message board design. It was designed to be able to ‘follow all your interests in one place’ and support free speech.

The company raised up to 1.5 million dollars over a period of four years during which time a small team of half a dozen programmers worked to completed the project deliverables.

The scope of the works was primarily to redesign the tired and plain tabled content styled format of traditional forum message board communities, with a modern social media look and feel coupled with a host of new features not provided for or supported by the then forum marketplace leaders.

This project was promoted as a one stop platform for any and all forum subjects, hosting thousands of forums all designed to be viewed from the users prospective, by providing the user with a host of features to personalize their user experience. For each forum topic they followed users would be able to customize the following;

    (a) wallpapers (upload their own wallpapers or use site default wallpapers)
    (b) have wallpapers change on regular rotation
    (c) theme colors used on feeds panels, panel headers and buttons / icons
    (d) filter their forum feeds by location of country, state, region
    (e) group multiple subjects into one or more different feeds groups for viewing
       
In addition their would be many other options available for any forum members to utilities that did not previously exist in the more traditional forum message boards of the day. Rather than just being able to post a question or reply, users would be able to do any of the following for each forum community;

    (a) post a blog series, 
    (b) post a vlog series.
    (c) post articles, 
    (d) post product reviews,
    (e) post and promote events via the forums 'coming events calendar' 
    (f) post interactive pinned map locations against posted content
    (g) post and upload videos
    (h) follow other forum users posts and content
    (i) subscribe to any posted content creators
    (j) direct message other forum users

For example with a surfing forum, when you post you would select a header that would let the community know if you were posting ‘a blog’ or ‘product review’ etc. Users would be able to easily identify in their feed the type of post being seen by the header title. In addition their was a ’pop up area’ design to view all these additional types of posts. For example you could go and search for all ‘blogs’ or ‘product reviews’ that had been submitted to the surfing forum, and refine that search by key words from either the post title or the content from within the posted material itself. 

As another example, a forum user may choose to start doing product reviews ‘unboxings’ etc as a series. Other forum members would be able to subscribe and be alerted whenever new content was posted by the creator doing the ‘product reviews’, and thus the content creator would be able to build a following of their own within the forum community. Similarity a forum member may create surfing vlog series (possibly on a round country surfing adventure) and forum users could subscribe and follow the content being created (akin to starting a you tube channel). 

Another example using ‘pinned map locations’ would allow forum users when posting, to submit a pin location on a world map related to their post content. Other forum users would be able to view the world map and zoom into a location of interest (somewhere they may plan on traveling for example) and using the pinned locations, start viewing all the relevant content that had been posted to the forum related to that area.

Essentially this project was designed to be an all in one platform for any material to do with any forum subject matter, with the user being able to filter and theme via customizing tools all their forum content from their own prospective to enhance their viewing experience. 

<h3>Follow and create forums:</h3>

Users would be able to choose to follow from thousands of per-existing forums, and or create their own forums for their own communities. Forums that users created for their own communities would be as follows

    (a) free to create
    (b) create forum within minutes using unique forum creation tool
    (c) be hosted for free on the platform
    (d) be able to earn money from advertising displayed on their forum
    (e) be able to have advertising provided by the hosting platform or arrange their own
       
The idea being that owners of forums communities would not have to pay any monthly charges for hosting and or have to deal with the ongoing management of their hosting needs, while also having access to a richer array of features to offer to their community members, all presented with a modern social media look and feel. Further this would provide forum owners an opportunity to gain access to a broader range of new users that followed other interests on the platform as a whole.

<h3>For comparison:</h3>

I appreciate that a text description of a visual format may be difficult to clearly understand, so for simplicity for current day comparison, think ‘Reddit’ but on steroids with loads more features, and an array of customization tools putting the user in control of their viewing experience. Having said that even to this day ‘Reddit’ itself does not in any way compare to the project that was being developed. ‘Reddit’ is more comprised of feeds designed for entertainment, rather than traditional forums styled table content that’s divided and arranged into a hierarchy of categories. To be clear this is not a criticism of ‘Reddit and this project was not designed to compete with ‘Reddit’ as it is a different beast entirely, however ‘Reddit’ may be arguably considered the closest comparison that exists in the current marketplace. 

<h3>The business model:</h3>

In short the site would be funded by advertising.

The platform aimed to only ever provide advertising directly related to the content material of each forum (eg: surfing ads for surfing forums, and gardening ads for gardening forums). The platform would simply take a modest percentage of ads across all forums which would be sufficient to fund all hosting and ongoing site development as necessary. This way the user is only subject to relevant advertising at the time that they are actively engaged in the related subject matter. This was considered best for the both the user and the advertiser. Surfing forum users don't mind seeing surfing ads in a surfing forum (just like seeing surfing ads in a surfing magazine), in fact they enjoy seeing the latest offers, products, media and such that is related to their topic of interest, it is far more appropriate than seeing an ad 'for an internet company','insurance company' or ‘new dating app’ just because their is someone in the forum to view the ad.

This method also meant that users data didn't have to be scrapped or sold, being that the platform itself would already know thorough the sign up process of creating a user account the basic relevant data required to target ads such by, age, location, country, and interests. It would be easy for advertisers to simply select the areas, gender and age group, and forum interest they wanted to promote to, all which would be handled through the platform itself so no customer data (email or other information otherwise would be offered or on sold).

In addition it is an important metric to understand that one user of the platform would act as many users from an advertising model point of view. For example if on average one user followed 6 different topics of interests, then 10 million site users would equate to 60 million forum users from an advertising prospective. With cost effective and targeted subject related advertising this would show to be a powerful and profitable feature of the site works.

<h3>To make a long story short:</h3>

The company raised up to 1.5 million in investment over a period of 4 years during site development. We hired up to half a dozen programmers during this time and went through the standard stages of development, re-design, further development, beta release/s, re-design more development, and this process was repeated numerous times.

We went through the expected ups and downs of early development phases involved for such an ambitious project. We got 90 percent of the main project deliverables completed, and experienced investment / funding issues along the way, and with the onset of Covid lockdown in the year 2020 unfortunately (without the project being completed) the development of the site works came to a grinding halt. For the last five years since the code has been sitting on the shelf collecting dust so to speak. 

<h3>Decision going forward:</h3>

The company has not been in a position to complete the project since due to a lack of financial capacity, and in consideration of other reasons that I won’t detail here, I have decided to make the source code ‘open sourced’ and available to the general public.

I appreciate usually there would be some type of commercial licensing agreement attached to such, however for principled reasons I have chosen to make it freely available for commercial use to the general public.

In addition to making the code base ‘open sourced’, the company has also officially relinquished any previously held rights and or ownership over both the ‘Source Code’ and ‘Intellectual Property’ of the intended site works. This simply means that both the ‘Source Code’ and ‘Intellectual Property’ are no longer assets that are held by the company that originally created them, and that all rights have been released and distributed to the public for free use with no licensing or commercial restrictions.
